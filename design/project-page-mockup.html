Overview

No single engine works efficiently from a standing start to Mach 6. This study sizes a serial three-engine system and checks it against every mission requirement with a coupled trajectory and cycle-analysis model.

Propulsion architecture

Each engine hands off where the next one becomes more efficient.

M 0 → 1.8
Afterburning turbofan
Low bypass (0.4) keeps thrust through the transonic regime.
M 1.8 → 4.0
Ramjet
Ram compression only, flown below 20 km for mass flow.
M 4.0 → 6.0
LOX/RP-1 rocket
66 s burn climbs to the 30 km release point.

Takeaways

Density beats Isp here

RP-1 tanks are about 6× smaller than LH2 for a short 660 m/s rocket burn.

Air-breathing does the work

Three-quarters of total impulse comes before the rocket lights.

Mature hardware

Every subsystem is flight-proven, unlike SABRE's precooler.
